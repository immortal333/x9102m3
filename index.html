Ticket validity
ViborgUNG is an inexpensive ticket for everyone aged 16 through 26. The ticket can be used on all city buses, local buses, and regional buses within Viborg Municipality — around the clock. The ticket cannot be used on DSB trains, or GoCollective trains.

Valid for city buses, local buses, and regional buses within Viborg Municipality. All young people from age 16 through 26 can buy ViborgUNG. ViborgUNG is not valid for travel across the municipal border. The ticket cannot be used on DSB or GoCollective trains. If you travel outside the municipality, you must buy an adult ticket for the part of the journey that is outside the municipality.
